# Get Help and Contact Support

## Choose the right place

- **Help articles:** setup instructions and troubleshooting
- **Feedback Portal:** feature requests, roadmap items, and Product Updates
- **Messages:** direct help with a current problem
- **Email:** support@escapedirector.com

## Include with a support request

- Room name
- Page or feature
- What you expected and what happened
- Browser and operating system
- Whether Live View, media, or offline readiness was involved
- A screenshot or recording when it does not expose guest or account information

Never send a password, payment information, access token, or customer data.
